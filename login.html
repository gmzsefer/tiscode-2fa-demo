How to try the demo
  1. Install the TISCODE app on your phone.
  2. Open the app and grant all the permissions it asks for.
  3. Tap the activation button in the app.
  4. On this computer, log in and choose TISCODE. Turn the sound up.
  5. Keep the phone near the computer while the sound plays.
  6. The login completes by itself. If the phone shows a notification and the page keeps waiting, tap the notification.
